Company FAQ — Acme Learning Corp.

Q: What does Acme Learning Corp do?
A: Acme Learning Corp builds open-source educational tools that help developers
   learn new technologies through hands-on projects. Our flagship product is
   "LearnByDoing", a CLI platform that generates guided coding exercises.

Q: Where is Acme headquartered?
A: We are headquartered in Hanoi, Vietnam, with remote team members across
   Southeast Asia, Europe, and North America.

Q: What is the return policy?
A: All our products are free and open-source, so there is no return policy.
   If you find a bug, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: Email support@acmelearning.example.com or join our Discord community at
   discord.gg/acmelearning (fictional link for this demo).
